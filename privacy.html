We retain your data for as long as necessary to provide our services and meet legal obligations. Contact and payment records are kept for the legally required period under Spanish tax law (typically 5 years). You may request deletion at any time.

Your rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Request restriction of processing.

To exercise any of these rights, email kamil@oedon.dev.
